    Frequently asked questions

    Do we have to replace Excel or our existing system?

    Not necessarily. Suitable existing systems can be retained and connected where useful. Whether another solution is needed depends on the workflow.

    Do you only offer Power BI?

    No. Barry Data & Analytics combines data analytics and business intelligence with AI engineering, process automation and custom software and web development. The task determines the tools.

    Does the checklist constitute consulting?

    No. The questions offer initial orientation. A substantiated recommendation requires clarification of the actual data, systems and requirements.

    How does a project begin?

    Briefly describe your report or workflow in the contact form. We can then clarify whether and which next step is appropriate. Paid services are agreed separately.

    Which report repeatedly takes time to prepare?

    Briefly describe the workflow, systems and desired result. Please do not send passwords or confidential company files in the initial enquiry.
